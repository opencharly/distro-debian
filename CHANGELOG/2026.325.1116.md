# 2026.325.1116 — fix(debian-skills): drop the copy step; kind:skill installs the owned skills

## Summary

The `debian-skills` carrier drops its `copy: .agents/skills` step, because charly now installs the skills a composed candy owns (opencharly/sdk#598; charly `v2026.325.1102`, opencharly/charly#1230).

That step was resolved against the carrier's own staged directory, so it could never reach the repo-root bodies (opencharly/sdk#444). Its own header said so: "PRE-EXISTING BROKEN AND IS TRACKED, NOT HIDDEN". Now `kind:skill` installs the body of every skill an image's composed candies own, at the image user's `~/.agents/skills/<name>/`, through one `COPY` of the owned content. It carries the same set whole in the `ai.opencharly.skill` label.

The carrier keeps its `skill:` routing nodes and its `skill-bodies-installed` ADE `check:`, which proves the bodies landed. Its header comment and `description:` now say whose install it is.

| file | change |
|---|---|
| `candy/debian-skills/charly.yml` | the `install-skill-tree` `run:` step deleted; header and `description:` rewritten |

**Order:** it needs charly at or above `v2026.325.1102`, which is merged and tagged.
